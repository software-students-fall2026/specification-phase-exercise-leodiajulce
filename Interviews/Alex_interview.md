## Observations and Feedback

**Navigation (strength).** Alex found the UI fairly intuitive to navigate as a student.

**Sharing (weakness).** The share button is broken. Ilyas reported the same issue independently.

**Local PDF export (gap).** Alex wants to download decks as PDFs and keep them locally, mainly so he can upload them to ChatGPT, the LLM he uses.

**Joining classes (gap).** Alex's biggest request. Students should be able to join a class from their homepage and see every lecture deck their professor has given there, instead of browsing or hunting for the professor's folder each time. If several of his professors used the app, he would join each of their classes and see all slides for each class in one place. He said this would help when going back over material. He also thought class-based organization would help professors, since organizing by project is possible today but difficult and not intuitive.

**Play deck aloud (strength).** Alex liked playing a deck aloud because it gives him a second run through the lecture.

**Full lecture transcript for students (gap).** Alex wants to be able to get the transcript of the entire lecture as a student. He would give it to his LLM so it is fully up to date on what was said in lecture and can tutor him: an AI teacher that does not go overboard and refers to what was actually said.

**Repetitive slide layouts (weakness).** Slides felt repetitive: almost always a title plus bullet points, which is boring to study from as a student. From the lecturer's side, he disliked that the layout has to be changed manually, slide by slide. He wished the app could infer the best slide layout from what is being said.
