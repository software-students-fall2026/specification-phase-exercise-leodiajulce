## Observations and Feedback
**Speech to slides (weakness).**
- This was his biggest problem.
- It stopped in the middle, ignored what he said, and worked differently for different topics.
- It worked in class for a professor, but kept failing for him.
- He thinks it is an API or connection problem, not Wi-Fi.
- He wants this fixed before new features are added.

**Text layout (weakness).**
- Text is stuck on the left.
- He cannot move or center it.
- He wants to move and resize text boxes like in PowerPoint.

**Two export buttons (weakness).**
- The export button shows up in two places.
- He wants just one.

**Privacy (weakness).**
- Users can see other people's slides without permission.
- There are no sharing settings.
- He wants slides private by default, with a public/private switch or a share button.

**Homepage (weakness).**
- "About Us" is hidden at the bottom.
- The page does not use the full screen width.
- He wants important sections at the top.

**Mobile (weakness).**
- The app works best on desktop.
- The mobile version has the same problems.

**No trash bin (gap).**
- Deleting is permanent, so work can be lost by mistake.
- He wants a trash bin that keeps deleted slides for 30 days.

**Languages (gap).**
- Only 5 languages are supported.
- He wants at least 9: English, Chinese, Spanish, French, Russian, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, and Bengali.
- NYU has campuses around the world (like Ghana and Abu Dhabi), so those languages matter.

**Search and help (gap).**
- There is no search or help menu.
- He pointed to PowerPoint's search and AI help as a good example.

**Handwritten math (gap).**
- The app cannot read handwritten formulas.
- The pen and highlighter tools are only a small workaround.
- He sees this as a future feature.
