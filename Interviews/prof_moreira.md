# Alan Moreira Slide Machine Interview
## Stakeholder Bio

- **Background:** Professor of Finance at NYU; teaches Data Driven Investing w AI & Python
- **User type:** Professor
- **Format:** In person, 2026-09-23. 
## Observations and Feedback

**Overall concept (strength):** The professor found the idea interesting and could see value in generating slides dynamically from what is being said during a lecture, though he noted that how useful it is would depend on the type of class.

**Editing generated slides (strength):** He liked that generated slides can be edited afterward, including through commands, rather than being locked into what the AI initially produces.

**Expected reference notes to be visible (weakness):** The professor initially expected the PDF or slides he uploaded as reference material to appear in the presentation interface while he was lecturing. Instead, the app uses them behind the scenes to generate new slides. This was confusing because his natural workflow would be to lecture from his existing notes, likely keeping them open on another screen. 


**Unreliable picture generation (weakness):** The AI did not consistently generate supporting photos when they were relevant to what the professor was discussing. Sometimes formulas appeared automatically and sometimes they did not, making the behavior difficult to predict.


**Prioritization controls (gap).:** The professor wants a way to tell the AI what information is important and what is not before or during slide generation. This would be particularly useful for formulas, figures, and other material that should be prioritized when the AI decides what belongs on a slide.


**Separate image library for lectures (gap):** He would like to upload a collection of images or figures separately from the main PDF and have the AI automatically retrieve the relevant image as he talks about it. His ideal workflow would let him provide both a PDF and a bank of pictures as reference sources separately, with the AI selecting and inserting the appropriate visual at the right point in the lecture.

**Ability to edit which photo is selected (gap - cascading from the gap above):** He would like to be able to click on the photo the AI automatically introduces and change it from the bank of photos he uploaded initially.
