# Prof. Bobbie Slide Machine Interview
## Stakeholder Bio

- **School:** NYU-TULSA
- **Background:**
  - Has used PowerPoint for a long time.
  - Writes in Word first, moves it to PowerPoint, cuts text, adds images, then saves as PDF.
  - Just started learning Canva.
  - Uses YouTube links instead of putting videos in slides.
  - Not much experience with AI tools.
- **User type:** Professor / lecturer
- **Format:** Interview by Diallo, in person, 09/23/2026. The professor tried the app and made a slide on "Native Arts and AI".
## Observations and Feedback
**Rating.**
- The professor gave the app 6.5/10.
- They said their low AI experience might have made the score lower.

**Making slide text short (weakness).**
- This was their biggest concern.
- They usually write too much and have to cut it down by hand.
- They were not sure how the app turns speech into short slide text.
- They would switch from PowerPoint if the app did this well.

**Side menu (weakness).**
- The menu is on the side, not the top.
- This felt strange compared to PowerPoint.

**Toolbar looks like the browser (weakness).**
- The export and settings buttons look like browser and Gmail icons.
- This was confusing.
- They want the toolbar to look different from the browser.

**Formatting (weakness).**
- They could not find how to change background color, add images, or style text.

**Images (weakness).**
- They asked by voice for "a Native American artist engaging with an AI tool to create digital artwork".
- The app gave a random image.
- They had to search for a better image by hand.
