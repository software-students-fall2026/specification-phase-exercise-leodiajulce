- **Format:** Zoom, full recording and transcript (Wispr Flow)

## WisprFlow AI Summary:
Julian demoed Slide Machine, an AI tool that generates lecture slides live from speech, to an interviewee. Feedback centered on fixing export bugs, reducing text density, adding structure/styling, and clarifying the best-fit use case (lecturers and students over work presenters).

Product Walkthrough
- Julian demoed Slide Machine: Discover feed, a professor's version control deck, and read-aloud AI voice playback.
- Slides generate live from spoken lecture content; seed material (notes, images, prompts) can be uploaded upfront.
- Auto-translation into multiple languages is available; accuracy untested by Julian.
- Live editing, whiteboard mode with pause/resume, and per-slide refine/layout/AI options were shown.

Export & Review Feedback
- Ilyas wants to export decks (PDF, Google Slides) to review visually and feed into other AI tools.
- Share button and Google Slides export are currently broken.
- Ilyas asked for a notes-page view alongside slides so users can pick key points without wading through everything.

Slide Design & Structure
- Ilyas felt slides are too text-heavy; slides should emphasize visuals matched to speech, not capture every word.
- Requested sectioning/categorization and slide numbers to navigate long decks (example had 109 slides).
- Requested color-coding and styling so important points stand out; manual bold/italic/color controls also wanted.
- Image generation pulls from public annotated databases; no fine-grained image editing (e.g., 'more cellular detail').

Use Cases & Positioning
- Core value is for lecturers who don't want to pre-build slides; first class isn't disadvantaged since nothing needs pre-upload.
- Ilyas sees it as strongest for professors and for students recording lectures, weaker for prepared work presentations.
- Ilyas currently uses Granola to turn work meetings into summary emails.
- Julian suggested a lecturer mode where slide notes tick off as the speaker covers each point.

Other Functionality & Pricing
- Exit-quiz feature can generate a Google Form from deck content.
- Free plan gives 75 minutes/month with paid upgrades; Ilyas felt pricing seemed generous.
- LaTeX/math equations render poorly on slides.
